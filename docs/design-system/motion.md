# Motion

## Allowed Motion

- Header settle on load.
- Hero content entrance.
- Slow grid drift and ambient glow.
- Subtle data scan in the intelligence panel.
- Hover lift on cards and buttons.
- Small bar pulse in DIRT visuals.

## Constraints

- No bouncing, scroll-jacking, heavy dependencies, or large continuous blur animation.
- Motion must not be required to understand the page.
- `prefers-reduced-motion: reduce` disables practical animation timing.
