# Imagery Direction

## Production Visual Strategy

Phase 2 intentionally uses original repo-native visual systems instead of generic healthcare stock photography:

- Platform orbit: clinical practice at the center, ROOT as the operating layer, surrounded by revenue operations, credentialing, practice operations, technology, automation, analytics, and DIRT.
- DIRT command visual: radar, aging landscape, denial intelligence, recovery priority, and PracticeOps signals.
- Clinical Glass surfaces: restrained dark interface panels with mint, data-blue, and warm signal accents.
- Fallback ROOT SVG assets: temporary until official brand assets are accessible.

## Rules

- Do not use patient imagery, clinical scenes, or fabricated dashboards as evidence.
- Do not imply HIPAA, financial, compliance, payer, or clinical guarantees.
- Do not use external stock assets unless a specific licensed asset is approved and recorded in `docs/brand/external-asset-sources.md`.
- Keep visual language operational, premium, calm, and decision-oriented.
