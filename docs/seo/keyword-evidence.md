# Keyword evidence (2026-10-06)

Source: two owner-supplied research files, `ROOT-specialty-practice-keywords.csv` and `ROOT-keyword-evidence.csv`
(`data_source=google_seo_public`, "estimated/reference from public evidence", `coverage=limited`, source dates 2026-09-30 to 2026-10-06,
recency review required). A third file, a market-entry report, was **not** used as a source for any site copy: its claims are unpublished and
unverified.

## What this evidence is, and is not

- It is a **qualitative** reading of how much public content and competition exists around a phrase ("high / moderate / weak public evidence").
- It contains **no** search volume, difficulty, cost-per-click, ranking or conversion data. Nothing here may be quoted as demand or as a ranking.
- Its sources are third-party vendor and association pages. Their statistics (for example a "16-20%" denial-rate figure) and differentiation claims
  (for example "24/7") **must not** be repeated as ROOT claims.
- The keyword list below is **only** phrases present in those files or already in [page-keyword-map.md](page-keyword-map.md). No keyword was invented.

## Rules that follow

1. One primary intent per page; support it with natural examples and internal links, never repetition.
2. Treat this evidence as a prompt for questions to the owner, not as a content brief: segment and specialty pages need confirmed scope first.
3. Re-run the research with a volume source before spending money on ads or on a content plan; refresh dated entries (2026-09-30, 2026-10-04).
4. No unsupported statistics, rankings, guarantees or compliance statements in any copy derived from this research.
