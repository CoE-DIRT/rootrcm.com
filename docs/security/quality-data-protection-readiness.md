# Quality And Data Protection Readiness

ROOT is not claiming certification in this MVP. This document records the operating posture needed to prepare for future certification, audits, and healthcare data-protection maturity.

## Reference Standards

- ISO 9001: quality management system discipline, documented processes, performance review, corrective action, and continual improvement.
- ISO/IEC 27001: information security management system discipline, risk assessment, risk treatment, asset handling, access governance, and evidence.
- ISO/IEC 27701: privacy information management system discipline for accountable PII handling.
- ISO/IEC 27018: cloud PII protection guidance for public cloud processors and cloud-provider evaluation.
- ISO/IEC 42001: AI management system discipline for governed automation and AI-assisted workflows.
- HIPAA Security Rule: administrative, physical, and technical safeguard readiness for future ePHI workflows.

## MVP Controls Reflected In The Website

- Public website does not accept PHI.
- Contact and Diagnostic forms require no-PHI acknowledgement.
- CTA analytics events exclude form body text and sensitive content.
- Terms and privacy pages warn against public PHI submission.
- DIRT and automation language avoids unsupported AI, compliance, or revenue guarantees.
- Official certification is not claimed.

## Future Certification Workstream

- Appoint control owners for quality, security, privacy, AI governance, and vendor management.
- Create a risk register and evidence register.
- Define document control, change control, incident response, access review, supplier review, complaint handling, corrective action, and management review procedures.
- Define PHI-enabled intake only after agreements, secure transfer, approved storage, access controls, logging, retention, and deletion are implemented.
- Map controls against the selected standard before scheduling any audit.
