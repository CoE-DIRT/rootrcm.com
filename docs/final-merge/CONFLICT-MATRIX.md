## Scope resolution

Public DIRT-only capabilities selected for port: glass/metric/pipeline/note/state primitives, richer hero, triage expand/explain, bounded scenario toggle, flow/lifecycle/timeline, neutral comparison, role and expansion maps. Each has a specific destination in the route map; nothing is included just to reach parity with DIRT's much larger route tree.

ROOT-only capabilities retain behavior and become visually native: inquiry and fallback, six social links and outreach dock, cookie controls, consent-gated analytics/replay, experiment and intent infrastructure, content indexing, route media/provenance, SEO/static outputs and publication gates.

No production setting, domain, CNAME, backend, private configuration, push, PR or deployment is part of this staging request. The final implementation's later release review remains distinct from this document handoff.
