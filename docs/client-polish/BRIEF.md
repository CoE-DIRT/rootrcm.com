# ROOT Client-Presentable Polish Brief

## Outcome

Make `rootrcm.com` feel finished enough to show a prospective independent physician practice without apology or explanation.

This pass is about perceived quality, clarity, trust, visual coherence, and commercial confidence. It is not limited to fixing bugs. If a section feels weak, generic, sparse, crowded, repetitive, or unfinished, improve it.

## Creative freedom

Use the current merged ROOT × DIRT site as the starting point, not as a constraint. You may simplify, rearrange, strengthen, add, remove, or recombine visual sections and interactions when that clearly improves the finished experience. Prefer one strong solution over preserving mediocre existing structure.

Use the existing stack and approved assets where practical. New lightweight components or visual treatments are welcome when they materially improve the result.

## Preserve

Keep these facts and safeguards correct:

- ROOT is the commercial company; DIRT is its revenue-intelligence layer.
- Revenue Optimization Diagnostic: `$2,500 fixed`.
- Managed RCM: `from $1,500 onboarding`, approximately `5% of collections where appropriate`.
- DIRT recurring intelligence: `$1,500–$2,500/month`.
- No PHI on public surfaces.
- No fabricated clients, testimonials, certifications, outcomes, or live integrations.
- Consent/privacy, forms, public/private DIRT boundaries, and production gates must continue to work.

## What "client-presentable" means

A first-time visitor should quickly understand what ROOT does, why DIRT matters, what to do next, and why the company looks credible enough to trust with revenue operations.

The site should feel intentional at both 1366×768 and mobile, with strong hierarchy, useful proof, polished navigation, deliberate spacing, good media/product framing, consistent interaction states, and no obvious development leftovers.

Automated audits are inputs, not design authority. Verify findings in the rendered product and use judgment.

## Sequence

1. Codex: independent audit and implementation map.
2. Claude: primary implementation with broad creative latitude.
3. Cursor: browser-led visual refinement and responsive polish.
4. Copilot: release hardening, review, CI, merge, deploy.

After this pass is founder-approved, move immediately to SEO + copywriting/content injection, then the content engine.
