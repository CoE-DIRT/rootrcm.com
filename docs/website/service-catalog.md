# Service Catalog

ROOT service pages are generated from `src/siteData.js`.

## Revenue Operations

- Revenue Cycle Management: managed billing, posting, denials, A/R, reporting, and controls.
- Medical Billing: clean-claim workflow, claim submission, rejection cleanup, payer follow-up, and escalation.
- A/R Recovery: aging segmentation, recoverability priority, payer follow-up, escalation, and recovery reporting.
- Denial Management: denial inventory, reason-code patterning, recovery workflow, preventability, and root-cause recommendations.
- Payment Posting: ERA/manual posting, reconciliation controls, exception tracking, and reporting.
- Patient Balances: patient-responsibility workflow, statement cadence, follow-up, exception handling, and reporting.

## Practice Operations

- Credentialing: provider enrollment, revalidation, payer follow-up, roster maintenance, and status visibility.
- Practice Operations: workflow design, ownership model, KPI cadence, escalation, and operating controls.
- Operational Consulting: assessment, roadmap, workflow redesign, operating cadence, and implementation support.

## Technology

- Healthcare IT: EHR/PM workflow alignment, permissions, support model, integrations, and data reliability.
- Workflow Automation: automation opportunity review, workflow design, pilot, controls, and monitoring.
- Reporting & Analytics: KPI definitions, source-data review, dashboard design, operating cadence, and decision register.

## Pricing Boundaries

- Diagnostic: `$2,500 fixed fee`.
- Credentialing: `$750-$1,500/provider initial`; `$150-$300/provider/month maintenance` when scoped.
- DIRT/Data Intelligence: `$2,500-$7,500/month`.
- Projects: `$5,000-$25,000` when scoped.
- Managed RCM and Full MSO: custom by scope.
