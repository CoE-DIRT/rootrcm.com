# Solution Architecture

ROOT Phase 2 organizes solutions by practice problem, not by internal service line.

## Solution Pages

- Revenue Leakage
- Aging A/R
- Denials
- Credentialing Bottlenecks
- Operational Efficiency
- Reporting Visibility
- Scaling Practice Operations

## Pattern

Each solution page follows the same narrative:

- Problem: what leadership feels and why the symptom exists.
- ROOT response: how ROOT works across revenue, operations, technology, and DIRT.
- Connected services: service lines that can own the work.
- DIRT contribution: how the intelligence layer improves prioritization.
- CTA: Diagnostic first, or Talk to ROOT for broader need.

## Platform Relationship

The solution pages ladder back to the platform architecture:

- Clinical Practice remains the center.
- ROOT is the business operating layer.
- DIRT is the intelligence layer inside ROOT.
- Diagnostic is the front-door assessment.
