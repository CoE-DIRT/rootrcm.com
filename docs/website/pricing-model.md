# Pricing Model

Pricing language is intentionally directional and must avoid unsupported guarantees.

## Published Models

- Revenue Optimization Diagnostic: `$2,500 fixed fee`.
- Managed RCM: monthly plus performance-aligned where appropriate.
- DIRT/Data Intelligence: `$2,500-$7,500/month`.
- Projects and Automation: `$5,000-$25,000 scoped projects`.
- Credentialing: `$750-$1,500/provider initial`; `$150-$300/provider/month maintenance` when scoped.
- Full MSO Partnership: custom.

## Rules

- Do not guarantee collections, denial reduction, cash acceleration, compliance, payer outcomes, or reimbursement results.
- Tie fees to written scope, volume, specialty, system complexity, and operating ownership.
- Keep Diagnostic positioned as evidence before bigger decisions.
- Keep Managed RCM, DIRT, projects, credentialing, and Full MSO positioned as optional next steps.
