PURPOSE
Use this checklist to prepare a deidentified revenue-cycle operating review.
ROOT is a healthcare revenue intelligence and operating partner; this worksheet
defines evidence needs, not a guaranteed financial result.

1. BASELINE AND PERIOD
[ ] Specify the reporting period, service dates, and comparison period.
[ ] Record total allowed amounts, contractual adjustments, payments, and reversals.
[ ] Document collection rate numerator and denominator explicitly.
[ ] Record gross/net days in A/R calculations and excluded balances.

2. A/R SEGMENTATION
[ ] Segment outstanding A/R by age band, payer, specialty, and ownership.
[ ] Record counts and aggregate dollar balances only; do not include patient IDs.
[ ] Distinguish actionable follow-up from nonrecoverable balances.
[ ] Identify filing deadlines, pending responses, and unapplied cash.

3. DENIAL ROOT-CAUSE TAXONOMY
[ ] Eligibility / coverage / coordination-of-benefits failure.
[ ] Prior authorization / referral mismatch.
[ ] Coding, modifier, documentation, or medical-necessity denial.
[ ] Credentialing, roster, NPI, taxonomy, or payer enrollment exception.
[ ] Timely filing, administrative payer error, duplicate, or underpayment.
[ ] Assign accountable process owner and a measurable correction date.

4. PAYER AND CONTRACT REVIEW
[ ] Compare allowed and paid at a consistent service/code level.
[ ] Confirm fee schedule and contract effective dates.
[ ] Check takebacks/recoupments in the original accounting period.
[ ] Document payer response and evidence for disputes.

5. IMPLEMENTATION AND AUDIT TRAIL
[ ] Assign one owner, next action, due date, and review status per issue class.
[ ] Record validation of payment only after ERA/EOB and bank reconciliation.
[ ] Recalculate the same KPIs on a consistent comparison cohort.
[ ] Disclose methodology, exclusions, unresolved issues, and assumptions.

PRIVACY BOUNDARY
Do not place PHI, patient identifiers, claim IDs, dates of birth, or chart details
in this worksheet or submit them through ROOT's public website. Use deidentified,
aggregate reports for initial consultation. Any later PHI exchange requires an
approved agreement and secure channel.
